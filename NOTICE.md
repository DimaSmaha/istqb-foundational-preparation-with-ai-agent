## How the material is used here

- Individual sample-exam questions are **extracted and regrouped** by learning objective into per-lesson
  practice blocks, per-chapter practice exams and blueprint-weighted mock exams. Each question is tagged
  with its source (for example `Exam A · Q1 · FL-1.1.1 · K1`). No sample exam is reproduced as a
  complete document in its original form.
- Sample Exam B is **not** included. `exams/0024-sample-exam-b.html` only explains how to generate an
  interactive copy locally from your own downloaded PDFs. The generated `*.local.html` file is
  git-ignored and is for personal study only.
- Lessons explain the syllabus content in the author's own words and quote short syllabus definitions,
  each citing its section (for example `§1.1`).
- Every lesson, exam and reference page carries a Copyright & Attribution footer pointing back to this
  file.
