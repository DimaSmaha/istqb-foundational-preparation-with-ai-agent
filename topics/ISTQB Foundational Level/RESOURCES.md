# ISTQB Foundational Level Resources

## Knowledge
All four documents are © ISTQB® and are **not included in this repository** — download them free from the
[official ISTQB® CTFL v4.0 page](https://istqb.org/certifications/certified-tester-foundation-level-ctfl-v4-0/)
and drop them into `cache/ISTQB/Foundational/` (the local, git-ignored location `istqb-prep` reads from).
Authors and conditions of use: see [`NOTICE.md`](../../NOTICE.md).

- **ISTQB® CTFL Syllabus v4.0.1** (`ISTQB_CTFL_Syllabus_v4.0.1.pdf`) — the sole source of taught concepts and definitions. All 6 examinable chapters, their keywords, and learning objectives (K1/K2/K3) live here. Every lesson cites the specific section (e.g. "§1.1") it draws from.
- **Sample Exam A v1.7 — Questions / Answers** (`ISTQB_CTFL_v4.0_Sample-Exam-A-Questions_v1.7.pdf`, `…-Answers_v1.7.pdf`) — 40 main questions + 26 bonus appendix questions (A1–A26), each tagged in the Answer Key with a Learning Objective code (e.g. `FL-1.1.1`) and K-level. Source of per-lesson practice questions.
- **Sample Exam C v1.6 — Questions / Answers** (`ISTQB_CTFL_v4.0_Sample-Exam-C-Questions_v1.6.pdf`, `…-Answers_v1.6.pdf`) — 40 questions, same LO-tagged Answer Key structure.
- **Sample Exam D v1.5 — Questions / Answers** (`ISTQB_CTFL_v4.0_Sample-Exam-D-Questions_v1.5.pdf`, `…-Answers_v1.5.pdf`) — 40 questions, same LO-tagged Answer Key structure.

## Wisdom (Communities)
Not used for this topic. The learner asked for content grounded only in the syllabus and sample exams; no community/forum sourcing has been done.

## Gaps
- No independent grading/practice-test platform is used — all practice questions come from the three local sample exams only, so question variety per topic is limited to whatever those three exams happen to cover.
- Community or peer-discussion resources (ISTQB forums, r/QualityAssurance, local study groups) have not been explored. Revisit if the learner later wants outside perspective or wants to discuss tricky questions with other candidates.
