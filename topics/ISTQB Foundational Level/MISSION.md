# Mission: ISTQB Foundational Level

## Why
Pass the ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL v4.0) exam through self-study, using only the official syllabus and sample exams already on hand — no outside courses or paid material.

## Success looks like
- Can explain and apply every examinable syllabus section (Chapters 1–6: Fundamentals of Testing, Testing Throughout the SDLC, Static Testing, Test Analysis and Design, Managing the Test Activities, Test Tools)
- Can correctly answer mock exam questions drawn from Sample Exams A, C, and D for each topic studied
- Can clear the six per-chapter practice exams, each pooling every LO-tagged question for its chapter, as the section-level check
- Can score at a passing level (65%, per the real CTFL exam) on the four 40-question mock exams — each drawn from the 146-question pool (Sample Exams A, C, D) and weighted to the official CTFL blueprint (8/6/4/11/9/2 questions across Chapters 1–6) — before attempting the real exam

## Constraints
- Self-directed learner, studying independently (not teaching anyone else)
- No fixed exam date — self-paced
- Content must be grounded only in the two local resources: `ISTQB_CTFL_Syllabus_v4.0.1.pdf` and the three Sample Exam A/C/D Questions+Answers PDFs, all under `cache/ISTQB/Foundational/`. No web search, no outside textbooks.
- Lessons are built chapter by chapter, not all at once

## Out of scope
- Syllabus Chapter 0 (Introduction) — explicitly not examinable
- Appendices A–C and the Index — not examinable
- Higher-level ISTQB certifications (Advanced/Expert levels)
- Community/wisdom-building (forums, study groups) — not requested by the learner
