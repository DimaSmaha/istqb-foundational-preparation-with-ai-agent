Chapter 5 Practice Exam — Managing the Test Activities

A submit-at-end practice exam covering all of Chapter 5 (§5.1–§5.5): test planning (test plan content, entry/exit criteria, estimation techniques, test case prioritization, the test pyramid, and the testing quadrants), risk management (risk level, project vs. product risks, product risk analysis and control), test monitoring/control/completion (metrics, test reports, communicating status), configuration management, and defect management (preparing defect reports). 20 questions, real CTFL passing mark of 65%. Nothing is graded or revealed question-by-question — answer everything, then click Submit Exam to grade the whole thing at once.
